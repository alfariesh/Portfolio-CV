## the brief

A portfolio for a designer who works with agents should show how the work gets made, not only how it ends up. So this site is a case study of itself: every decision on it was made on a canvas, built with an agent, and reviewed by me.

## the approach

The layout started in Paper: one 1082 px grid of bordered cells, with small registration marks where the lines meet. An early version also had a full graph-paper grid behind everything. It looked right on the canvas and busy on a real screen, so it went. The rule now is simple: the only lines are cell borders, registration marks and the rulers on the edge.

The paper itself is a WebGL fibre texture behind the footer and the notes, with folds and crumples turned off so it adds grain but never lines. You can play with it in [paper texture, zero folds](/notes/paper-texture-zero-folds).

Case studies and notes are MDX files in the repo. Moving between pages uses React's `ViewTransition` on top of the Navigation API, so a project's illustration travels with you from the home page into its case study.

## what shipped

- a single React 19 app on Vite+, with case studies and notes as MDX
- page transitions that carry illustrations and covers between pages
- a pre-commit hook that formats, lints and type-checks every change
