## the brief

Saving small amounts is easy to start and easy to forget. Chicken Bank turns one goal, like a new bike, into something you can see filling up, and makes topping it up a single tap.

The audience counts in rupiah, so the numbers are long. A goal of Rp 3.800.000 is a normal case, not an edge case, and the design had to be built around that.

## the approach

The prototype used real data from the start. Realistic amounts broke the first layouts straight away, which was the point: the progress ring, the goal card and the top-up chips were all sized for eight-digit numbers before any of them got polished.

The ring is the hero of the app. Everything else is quiet: one goal per screen, quick amounts of 50k and 100k, round-ups in the history. The playful part lives in the motion and the haptics, not in extra decoration.

I prototyped on the canvas, then rebuilt the app natively in SwiftUI, because the springs and haptics were the product, and they had to feel right in the hand.
