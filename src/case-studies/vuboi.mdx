## the brief

Most planners compete for your attention. Vuboi was meant to do the opposite: show the few rituals that make up a day, how long each one takes, and then get out of the way.

I owned both the design and the build, so the real brief was to keep the two halves honest with each other. Nothing could be designed that I wasn't willing to maintain on two platforms.

## the approach

I started on the canvas with real content instead of placeholder text: actual ritual names, real durations, the awkward long titles. The home screen went through many versions before it lost everything that didn't help someone start their morning. No streaks, no badges, one featured routine and a short list for today.

The visual system is small on purpose. Two type sizes do most of the work, colour lives in the thumbnails, and the chrome stays ink on paper. Every value is a token, defined once on the canvas and synced to the single theme file that both the iOS and the Android build read.
