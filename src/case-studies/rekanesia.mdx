## the brief

Rekanesia needed a visual language for fields, deliveries, factories and paperwork, plus a clear way to show where every record stands. The icons and the status labels had grown one at a time, and it showed.

## one grid

Every pictogram is drawn on the same 24 px grid with a 1.5 stroke and sharp corners. Two keylines, a 20 px circle and an 18 px square, keep round and square shapes the same visual size, and a 2 px safe area keeps strokes off the edge.

The set is small on purpose: a leaf, a drop, a truck, a factory, a sack, a chart, and a few more. Each one had to read at 16 px in a table and at 36 px in an empty state, so details that only worked large were removed.

## states

Draft, in review, approved, rejected, archived. Each state is a token, not a one-off colour: `--state-draft`, `--state-review` and so on, each with a background, a text colour and a dot. A screen never picks its own green.

Writing the states down as a vocabulary also settled arguments that used to happen in every review. "Pending" and "waiting" are the same state, so only one of them exists.
